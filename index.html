OUR PURPOSE

Better connections make a stronger food system.

HarvestHub is a student project concept designed to make crop information easier to discover and direct farmer–buyer communication more accessible.

  • ✓ More visibility for local farmers
  • ✓ Easier crop discovery for buyers
  • ✓ Clear listing details and asking prices
